Why Nivult

Built for teams that verify before they buy

Most data vendors ask you to trust the number. Nivult shows you the receipt on every field, so your analysis survives the question "where does this come from?"

⛓

Per-field provenance

Every value carries its source: companies_house, sirene, gleif, pdl. We publish our coverage gaps openly. A declared gap protects your analysis; a hidden one becomes your refund.

◎

Direct from employers

No aggregators, no social scraping. A posting is marked closed only when it disappears from the employer's own page, never because it grew old. 654,113 verified closures are preserved in history.

◈

Models we measure

Job family, seniority, contract type, remote policy and technologies come from our own classifiers, scored class by class against hand labeled benchmark sets. The per-class scores ship with the data, not with the marketing.

✦

Legal identity, at scale

Legal name, legal form, registration number and founding year from public business registries across Europe, North America and Oceania. This is the layer that job board resellers cannot sell you.

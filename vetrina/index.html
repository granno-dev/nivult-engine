Why Nivult

Built for teams that verify before they buy

Most data vendors ask you to trust the number. Nivult declares source metadata for selected enriched fields and publishes field coverage, so you can evaluate the data before you buy.

⛓

Declared provenance

Selected enriched values include their source — sirene, companies_house, gleif, pdl. Coverage is published for a documented set of fields; gaps are declared rather than hidden.

◎

Employer and public sources

No commercial aggregators or social scraping. Sources include employer career systems and public employment portals. Closure timestamps reflect Nivult's source-checking pipeline, not age alone. More than 3.6 million closures are preserved in history.

◈

Models we measure

Job family, seniority, contract type, remote policy and technologies come from classifiers evaluated class by class against hand-labeled benchmark sets. Exported records contain the resulting classifications; benchmark scores are not fields in the dataset.

✦

Official business registries

Legal name, legal form, registration number and founding year from public business registries across Europe, North America and Oceania — the layer that job-board resellers cannot sell you.

The delta feed

Snapshots are free. The film is the product.

Anyone can sell you a list of postings. The value is in what changed: which company just opened a new market, which role closed in nine days, which technology appeared for the first time. GET /v1/changes?since= delivers new, updated and closed postings from your last sync, every day.

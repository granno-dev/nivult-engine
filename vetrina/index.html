Why Nivult

Built for teams that verify before they buy

Most data vendors ask you to trust the number. Nivult shows the source on every field, so your analysis survives the one question that matters: «where does this come from?»

⛓

Per-field provenance

Every value carries its source — sirene, companies_house, gleif, pdl. Coverage gaps are published, not buried: a declared gap protects your analysis; a hidden one would be a refund.

◎

Direct from employers

No aggregators, no social scraping. A posting is marked closed only when it disappears from the employer's own page — never because it grew old. 654,113 verified closures are preserved in history.

◈

Models we measure

Job family, seniority, contract type, remote policy and technologies come from our own classifiers, scored class by class against hand-labeled benchmark sets. The per-class scores ship with the data, not with the marketing.

✦

Legal identity, at scale

Legal name, legal form, registration number and founding year from public business registries across Europe, North America and Oceania — the layer that job-board resellers cannot sell you.

A posting marked closed is a posting that is gone — that is the ground truth your risk models can rely on.

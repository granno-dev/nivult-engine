Per-field provenance

Every value carries its source: companies_house, sirene, gleif, pdl. We publish our coverage gaps openly, because a declared gap protects your analysis better than a hidden one.

◎

Direct from employers

No aggregators and no scraped social networks. A posting is marked closed only when it disappears from the employer's own page. 654,113 verified closures are preserved in history.

Models we measure

Job family, seniority, contract type, remote policy and technologies are classified by our own models, scored against hand-labeled benchmark sets. Per-class scores are published, not averaged away.

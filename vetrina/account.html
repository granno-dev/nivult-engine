Nivult API quickstart

          
Search live job postings and company records with JSON over HTTPS. Create an active key in API Keys, store it in the NIVULT_API_KEY environment variable, and send it as X-Api-Key. Never put a key in a URL or client-side code.

          
BASE URLhttps://api.nivult.com

          
Step 1 — Check your account for 0 credits

          
Call GET /v1/usage. It costs 0 credits and works even when your balance is zero.

          
Step 2 — Fetch up to five jobs

          
Call GET /v1/jobs?limit=5. That first request costs at most 5 credits because jobs cost 1 credit per returned record. The Python example caps itself at one page; increase max_pages only when you are ready to pay for more results.

          

          

          
Read a page

          
Jobs, companies and changes return data (an array), count_page (the number of records in this page) and next_cursor (a string or null). There is no total_count: keyset pagination avoids an expensive full-result count on every request.

          
{
  "data": [],
  "next_cursor": null,
  "count_page": 0
}

          
For a low-risk first query, keep limit=5. Use Coverage to understand field availability before building a large pipeline.

Pay per result, not per call. Empty results and errors cost 0 credits. The recurring plan or one-time trial allowance is spent before purchased credits. Top-ups never expire, but they do not unlock Delta or Daily bulk. Usage, coverage, the export manifest and bulk downloads are unmetered service endpoints; authentication, rate limits and entitlements still apply.

          
For direct paid endpoints, the balance is an effective page-size limit: at most floor(balance / credits_per_record) records can be returned. A balance of 12 allows 12 jobs or 4 companies. Below the cost of one result, the paid endpoint returns a credit 429. Direct accounts at or below 1,000 monthly credits also have a page cap of 25; higher-volume accounts can request up to 500.

        


        

          
Jobs

          
GET/v1/jobs

          
Returns active postings for 1 credit per returned job. Different parameters are combined with AND. Within a parameter, comma-separated values are ORed only where the table says so. Unknown enum-like values usually produce an empty result rather than a 400; source_type and has_salary are validated exceptions.

          
Search & Text

          
ParameterType and matchingReal example

            
qString; full text over title and description. All terms are ANDed.q=data platform engineer

            
q_anyString; up to 15 comma-separated alternatives, ORed. Terms inside each alternative are ANDed. Can be combined with q.q_any=MBSE,SysML,CATIA

            
titleString; full text restricted to title. All terms are ANDed.title=systems engineer

          

          
Role, Skills & Tech

          
ParameterType and matchingReal example

            
categoryString; comma OR over the 33 job families below.category=Data%20%26%20Analytics,Software

            
seniorityString; comma OR: intern, junior, mid, senior, lead, head.seniority=mid,senior

            
employment_typeString; comma OR: full_time, part_time, contract, temporary, internship, apprenticeship.employment_type=full_time,contract

            
departmentString; comma OR over exact source department values. There is no department discovery endpoint.department=Engineering

            
skillsString; comma OR, exact match against extracted skills. Broader and noisier than normalized technologies.skills=MBSE,SysML

            
technologyString; comma OR, case-insensitive exact match against normalized technologies.technology=SAP,Kubernetes

          

          
technology versus skills: use technology for curated, normalized tools such as SAP or Python; use skills for the wider extracted skill vocabulary such as MBSE. They are different fields, not aliases.

Location

          
ParameterType and matchingReal example

            
countryString; comma OR over ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes.country=DE,FR

            
cityString; comma OR over city values.city=Berlin,Paris

            
stateString; comma OR over state or region values.state=Berlin,Bavaria

            
remoteString; comma OR: remote, hybrid, onsite.remote=remote,hybrid

          

          
Company

          
ParameterType and matchingReal example

            
companyString; employer-name full text. All terms are ANDed.company=The Dixon Group

            
company_slugString; comma OR over exact employer slugs within the source.company_slug=danone

            
company_domainString; comma OR. Scheme, www and path are normalized away.company_domain=dixonvalve.com

          

          
Compensation

          
ParameterType and matchingReal example

            
salary_currencyString; comma OR over source currency codes.salary_currency=EUR,USD

            
has_salaryBoolean string, true or false; tests whether either observed extremum exists. Not implied by salary bounds.has_salary=true

            
salary_minNon-negative number; filters the record's observed salary_min >= N. Records with null salary_min do not match.salary_min=50000

            
salary_maxNon-negative number; filters the record's observed salary_max <= N. Records with null salary_max do not match.salary_max=80000

          

          
Observed amounts stay in source units. salary_min and salary_max are not annualized and are not normalized for gross or net pay. salary_period retains the source period, so compare period and currency before comparing amounts. Bounds do not implicitly add has_salary=true. Invalid salary numbers, negative values, or salary_min > salary_max return 400. Separate salary_estimate_* fields are annual gross Nivult benchmarks, not observed employer amounts.

          
Source & Dates

          
ParameterType and matchingReal example

            
atsString; comma OR over source slugs returned in records. There is no facets or ATS-discovery endpoint.ats=workday,greenhouse

            
exclude_atsString; comma OR of source slugs to exclude. Exclusion wins if a slug is also in ats.exclude_ats=jsonld,indeed

            
source_typeString; comma OR: direct, aggregator, staffing, public_board.source_type=direct,staffing

            
languageString; comma OR over posting language codes.language=en,it

            
posted_afterISO 8601 date or timestamp; inclusive (posted_at >= value).posted_after=2026-11-01T00:00:00Z

            
posted_beforeISO 8601 date or timestamp; exclusive (posted_at < value).posted_before=2026-12-01T00:00:00Z

            
dalLegacy string alias used only when posted_after is absent; same inclusive semantics.dal=2026-11-01

          

          
ATS alias: bundesagentur maps to bundesanstellung case-insensitively, token by token, including inside comma-separated lists such as ats=greenhouse,Bundesagentur. The normalization applies to both ats and exclude_ats. If the same slug appears in both parameters, directly or through the alias, exclusion wins.

          
Pagination

          
There is no total_count. Follow the opaque keyset cursor until it is null; start with limit=5 to cap initial spend and use Coverage for field availability.

          
ParameterType and matchingReal example

            
limitInteger, default 100, range 1–500. Tier and balance can lower the effective limit; limit=5 is a safe first request.limit=5

            
cursorOpaque string copied exactly from next_cursor. Keep the other filters unchanged.cursor=WyIyMDI2LTExLTI3VDA5OjAwOjAwIiwiMDFKQUJDMTIzIl0=

Companies

          
GET/v1/companies

          
Returns company records keyed by ats + company_slug for 3 credits per company. Company filters are single-value filters: they do not comma-OR. Different filters are ANDed.

          
ParameterType and matchingReal example

            
countryString; exact country of the company record, not every posting location.country=IT

            
industryString; exact industry value. There is no industry discovery endpoint.industry=Distribution of gaseous fuels

            
qString; case-insensitive substring search on company name.q=italgas

            
technologyString; case-insensitive exact match on the company-level normalized technology aggregate.technology=Kubernetes

            
employees_minInteger >= 0; employees >= N. Missing employee counts do not match.employees_min=500

            
employees_maxInteger >= 0; employees <= N. Missing employee counts do not match.employees_max=5000

            
limitInteger, default 100, range 1–500; tier and floor(balance / 3) can lower it.limit=25

            
cursorOpaque keyset string from next_cursor; keep filters unchanged.cursor=WyJJdGFsZ2FzIiwiYmFtYm9vaHIiLCJpdGFsZ2FzIl0=

          

          
When both employee bounds are present, employees_min <= employees_max is required. Negative values, non-integer values and inverted ranges return HTTP 400 with a string {"detail": "..."} error.

Changes / Delta feed

          
GET/v1/changes?since={ISO timestamp}

          
Synchronizes a local job index for 1 credit per returned event. It requires the Delta entitlement: an active Growth-or-higher subscription with at least 100,000 credits/month. Top-ups do not unlock it.

          
ParameterType and semanticsExample

            
sinceRequired ISO 8601 timestamp. Return events at or after this time within retained history.since=2026-11-27T08:00:00Z

            
limitInteger, default 100, range 1–500; tier and balance can lower it.limit=5

            
cursorOpaque keyset cursor for this response stream. It is not the next since.cursor=WyIyMDI2LTExLTI3VDA5OjAwOjAwIiwiMDFLQkMwTSJd

          

          
EventRecord semanticsClient action

            
newSparse discovery event with identity, source and selected posting fields; it is not a full job snapshot.Insert or fetch the full job if your pipeline needs enriched fields.

Rate limits & response headers

          
Rate limiting is separate from credit balance. Limits are enforced in process, per API instance, using a per-key identity for direct traffic and the RapidAPI user identity for marketplace traffic.

          
Channel and monthly volumeSustained rateBurstScope

There are no X-RateLimit-* headers. Leave client-side capacity for bursts and implement exponential backoff for temporary failures.

Errors & pagination

          
Errors and empty results cost 0 credits. Read the HTTP status and JSON body, fix the cause, then retry only when the table says retrying can help.

          
StatusMeaningWhat to do

            
400Invalid date, cursor, source_type, has_salary, salary number/range, invalid employees_min/employees_max values or range, too many q_any terms, or expired Delta retention.Correct the parameter. Salary and employee-range validation is 400. On delta_retention_exceeded, run a full resync.

            
401Missing, invalid or revoked API key.Send an active key in X-Api-Key; create a replacement if needed.

            
403Required Delta or Bulk subscription entitlement is missing.Upgrade to an eligible active plan; top-ups alone do not help.

            
404Company or export not found, or unknown export name.Check piattaforma + slug, use jobs or companies (or the legacy offerte/aziende aliases), or retry when the daily export is ready.

            
422FastAPI typed parameter validation failed, such as limit=0 or a non-integer limit.Fix types and constraints from the detail array. Salary and employee-range validation is 400, not 422.

            
429Rate limit or insufficient direct credits. There is no 402 on /v1.For rate limits, honor Retry-After and back off. For credits, stop immediately and never retry automatically; add credits or change plan first.

            
503Database, credit counter or query timeout.Retry with exponential backoff and jitter; narrow an expensive query if it times out repeatedly.

Paginate safely

          
  • Use next_cursor exactly as returned; it is an opaque keyset cursor, not an offset. Keep filters and limit unchanged while following it.
  • Stop when next_cursor is null. Treat a repeated non-null cursor as a client or server error rather than looping forever.
  • There is no total_count. Use count_page for page size, /v1/coverage for field fill rates, and start with limit=5 to control spend.
  • For Delta, keep the previous successful sync timestamp separately for the next since; a cursor is never a timestamp.
